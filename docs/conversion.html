Converting from the Earlier Contract

The contract in ops-contract.md replaced an earlier one. Documents written for the earlier contract are not valid and are not "compatible"; they are converted. This guide is the map. It is a historical reference, not an onboarding path: new work starts from practice.md.

The earlier contract and everything retired with it stay available in Git history. The last commit before the reformulation is ca82040.

How to convert a prompt

  1. Run the validator on the old file. Each legacy-* message names the field and its replacement.
  2. Map the fields with the tables below. Preserve what the task is for; do not carry over what had no meaning.
  3. Write what the old format could not say: an objective that can be checked and at least one verification check.
  4. Report what you decided and what you could not map. Do not relabel an unknown actor silently, and do not pick a side when persona and agent disagree.
  5. Validate the result.

The ops-author Skill follows this procedure. A complete worked example, with the legacy input taken verbatim from history, is in examples/conversion/onboarding/.

Persona catalogue to agent map

The ten personas (SystemIntegrator, SoftwareDeveloper, CodeAuditor, ProductStrategist, QAEngineer, DevOpsOrchestrator, AIEngineer, DocumentationCurator, Maintainer, Architect) are not replaced by ten agents. They described roles; none of them was ever a configured executor.

Why the spec layer was retired

The earlier design placed a specification, requirements, acceptance criteria, test cases, a traceability matrix and a conformance report between a request and the work. Its reference example ended in the verdict DEMO: every artifact validated, and no software existed. Six schemas, seven prompts and ten role labels were needed to say what one prompt can say with objective, requirements and verification, and to prove what one worklog entry proves with check results. The useful ideas survive: requirements have identifiers, each one needs a check, and completion needs evidence.
