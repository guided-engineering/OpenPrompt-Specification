## fix

Replaced `line.split(',').map(trim)` in `src/csv.mjs` with a left-to-right scan:

- For each field, skip leading whitespace. If the next character is `"`, the
  field is quoted: read until the closing quote, keeping commas and whitespace,
  and turning `""` into `"`. Text between the closing quote and the next comma
  is appended, trimmed (lenient handling of malformed input); an unterminated
  quote runs to end of line.
- Otherwise, the field is the raw slice up to the next comma, trimmed — the same
  operation as before, so quote-free lines behave identically (R4).

Signature `parseCsvLine(line)` and export name unchanged; no imports added.
